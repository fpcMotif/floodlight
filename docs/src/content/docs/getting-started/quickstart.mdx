Install Nanospot, grant file access on first launch, and open apps or copied items from one panel.

<Steps>

1. **Download**

   <DownloadButton label="Download" />

   Or [build from source](../../development/building/).

2. **Move it to Applications**

   Open `Nanospot.dmg`. Drag **Nanospot** onto **Applications**.

3. **Grant Full Disk Access**

   Without Full Disk Access, a small guidance panel opens beside **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access**. Drag the Nanospot card into the list to let the File Index scan protected folders in your home directory.

   Once granted, the card disappears and Nanospot scans your home directory in the background.

4. **Search**

   Press <kbd>⌥ Space</kbd> from any app. Start typing to match apps, files, folders, links, or web searches.

   - Press <kbd>Return</kbd> to open the selected row.
   - Use <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> to move through results.
   - When the field is empty, press <kbd>⌘ 2</kbd>–<kbd>⌘ 6</kbd> to open your most-used apps.
   - Press <kbd>Escape</kbd> or click outside the panel to dismiss it.

   <figure class="fl-doc-shot">
     <Image
       src={panelListScreenshot}
       alt="The Nanospot search panel showing Calendar as the top hit, filter chips, and file results."
     />
     <figcaption>Search apps, files, and web actions from one panel on <kbd>⌥ Space</kbd>.</figcaption>
   </figure>

5. **Open Clipboard History**

   Press <kbd>⌥ ⇧ C</kbd> from any app to open the clipboard board. Filter by **All**, **Text**, **Files**, or **Images**, then press <kbd>Return</kbd> to paste into the previous app.

   <figure class="fl-doc-shot">
     <Image
       src={panelBoardScreenshot}
       alt="The Nanospot clipboard board with history rows on the left and a preview on the right."
     />
     <figcaption>Review copied text, links, colors, code, images, and files on <kbd>⌥ ⇧ C</kbd>.</figcaption>
   </figure>

</Steps>
